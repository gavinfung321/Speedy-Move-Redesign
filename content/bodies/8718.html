table and Desk Disposal FAQ

Do you remove all types of tables and desks?

Yes, we remove all types of tables and desks, regardless of size, material, or design. Whether it’s a dining table, coffee table, office desk, or conference table, we are fully equipped to handle the removal. We also cater to more unique pieces like antique tables, adjustable desks, and workbenches. Our team will carefully assess the item to ensure safe handling during the removal process, no matter its shape or weight.

How do you ensure my walls and floors won’t be damaged during removal?

We take extra precautions to protect your property when removing furniture. Our team is trained to navigate through tight spaces and tricky areas, using specialized equipment like protective coverings for floors and padding for corners. We take time to plan the best route to avoid scratching walls, denting floors, or damaging doorways. Every step of the removal is designed to ensure your space is left in perfect condition once the job is done.

What happens to my old table or desk after it's removed?

Once your table or desk is removed, we assess whether it’s suitable for donation. If it’s in good condition, we’ll deliver it to local charities or nonprofit organizations, ensuring it finds a second home. For items that aren’t in a reusable state, we prioritize eco-friendly disposal methods. We recycle components such as metal, wood, and plastic to reduce landfill waste and support environmental sustainability. Our goal is to minimize the environmental impact at every step of the removal process.

Can you handle large and heavy tables or desks?

Yes, we specialize in the removal of large and heavy furniture. Whether it’s a massive conference table, a solid wood executive desk, or a heavy marble dining table, our crew is trained to safely disassemble and transport oversized items. We use the proper tools and techniques, including lifting straps, dollies, and protective coverings, to ensure the safe removal of even the heaviest pieces without causing any damage to your space or the furniture itself.

Do I need to prepare the table or desk before removal?

You don’t need to worry about preparing the furniture for removal. Our service is designed to be fully hands-off for you. We can disassemble complex items, such as office desks with built-in cabinets or adjustable-height desks. Just point us in the right direction, and our team will take care of everything from disassembly to hauling it out and loading it onto our trucks. You won’t need to lift a finger during the process.
