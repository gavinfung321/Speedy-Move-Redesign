Microwave Disposal and Removal FAQ

How does the microwave removal process work?

Our microwave removal process is simple and hassle-free. Once you schedule an appointment, our team will arrive at your home, carefully remove the microwave from your kitchen or any other location, and ensure there’s no damage to your property. We handle everything from start to finish, including loading the microwave onto our clean trucks and ensuring it is properly disposed of or recycled.

Do you offer same-day microwave removal services?

We understand the need for prompt service, and in many cases, we can offer same-day removal depending on our availability. We recommend booking as early as possible to secure a convenient time for you. Our team will work with you to accommodate your schedule and ensure the removal process is quick and seamless.

What happens to my old microwave after removal?

Once we remove your microwave, we assess whether it is still functional. If it is, we try to donate it to organizations or charities that can make good use of it. If the microwave is no longer operational, we focus on recycling its components like metal and plastic in an eco-friendly manner, helping to reduce waste and environmental impact.

Do I need to prepare my microwave for removal?

There’s no need for extensive preparation on your end. All we ask is that you ensure the microwave is unplugged and cleared of any personal items or food. Our team will handle everything else, including disconnecting any built-in units if necessary, and ensuring safe removal without any damage to your home.

Can you remove built-in microwaves or larger models?

Yes, we can remove built-in microwaves or larger, heavier models as well. Our team is trained to handle various types and sizes of microwaves, from countertop units to built-in ones. We take extra care in safely removing them without damaging surrounding cabinetry or walls.
