BBQ Grill Disposal and Removal FAQ

Do you disassemble BBQ grills before removal?

Yes, we offer full disassembly services for BBQ grills as part of our removal process. Whether it's a small portable grill or a large built-in unit, our team will carefully dismantle the grill for safe removal, ensuring no damage to your property.

What happens to my old BBQ grill after it’s removed?

If your BBQ grill is in good working condition, we prioritize donating it to local charities. If donation isn’t an option, we ensure the grill is responsibly recycled, reclaiming materials like metal and plastic to reduce waste and minimize environmental impact.

What do I do with old gasoline tanks?

Before you get rid of your old grill or BBQ, you’ll need to remove the propane tank. While Speedy Move can safely remove your old grill and other components, we’re often not able to remove old propane tanks. 
Many recycling depots, transfer stations, and landfills are able to accept old propane tanks for disposal. It’s best to check with your local recycling center first to see if they can be recycled in your area. If not, you may need to drop it off at a landfill or transfer station yourself.

How do I remove propane tanks from my BBQ?

Since we’re usually not able to remove propane tanks for you, we ask that you ensure the propane tank is fully disconnected before we remove it. Here are some tips on how to safely remove your propane tank:
1) Make sure your propane grill is turned off.
2) For a refillable tank, close the tank valve (turn it all the way to the right, or clockwise). Small disposable propane tanks don’t usually have valves, so you can simply unscrew the tank to remove it.
3) Once the valve is shut, twist off the threaded pressure regulator.
4) Remove the tank from the grill. (It may be secured with a bolt or screw—simply remove this to separate the tank.)  
5) Place your tank in a safe spot—do not store it indoors, even if it’s empty!

Do I need to clean the grill before removal?

While it’s not necessary to thoroughly clean the grill before removal, we recommend removing any loose debris or ashes for easier handling. Our team will take care of the rest, including disassembly and transportation.
