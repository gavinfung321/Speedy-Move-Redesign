Demolition FAQ

How Long Does a Demolition Project Usually Take to Complete?

✅ Detailed Timeline Analysis

Standard Residential (300-500 sq.ft):
✓ Basic demolition: 1-2 working days
✓ Including waste removal: +0.5 day

Commercial Space (500-1000 sq.ft):
✓ Office: 2-3 days (with system furniture removal)
✓ Restaurant: 3-4 days (including exhaust system dismantling)

✅ Key Factors Affecting Duration

  • Building structure (concrete walls require 50% more time than drywall)
  • Waste disposal (no service elevator adds 30% time for high-rises)
  • Work restrictions (some commercial buildings only allow after-hours work)

✅ Important Notes for Owners

  • Recommended: Allow 10-15% buffer time for unexpected delays
  • Available: Daily progress reports with site photos

How to Accurately Estimate Demolition Costs?

✅ Pricing Components

✅ Hidden Cost Alerts

  • Asbestos Inspection: $1500-3000 (Mandatory for pre-1985 buildings)
  • Night Work: 20-30% surcharge (Noise permit required)

✅ Cost-Saving Tips

  • Self-removal of recyclables (e.g. metal doors/windows) saves $3-5/sq.ft
  • 5-10% discount typically available in off-season (Jan-Mar)

Is Insurance Required for Demolition Projects?

✅ Essential Insurance Types

  • Third-Party Liability Insurance (Min. $5M coverage): Protects against damage to neighboring properties/public facilities
  • Workers' Accident Insurance: Legal requirement for on-site labor protection
  • Comprehensive Project Insurance (Optional): Covers fire, flooding, and other accidents

✅ Owner Checklist

  • Request valid insurance certificates from contractors
  • Verify policy coverage spans the entire project duration

How to Dispose of Old Furniture & Appliances?

✅ Eco-Friendly Recycling Options

Recyclables (metal/plastic/glass):

  • Processed by government-approved recyclers
  • Some charities accept donations (e.g. furniture/appliances)

E-waste (computers/TVs/AC units):

  • Handled by licensed recyclers per EPD regulations
  • Check community collection points or "Green@Community" program

✅ Non-Recyclable Waste

  • Delivered to designated government waste stations
  • Full compliance with environmental laws

✅ Important Notes

  • Verify item condition before donation
  • Large furniture requires self-arranged transport or pickup service

Is Relocation Necessary During Demolition?

✅ General Guidelines

Short-term Projects (1-2 days):

  • Occupants may stay but should seal off work areas
  • Sensitive individuals (e.g. children/elderly) may avoid active work hours

Large-scale Projects (3+ days):

  • Temporary relocation advised for extensive works/dust-heavy projects
  • Implement dust containment (e.g. room sealing) if remaining onsite

✅ Key Considerations

  • Coordinate special arrangements with contractors in advance
  • Expect noise/dust - prepare necessary protections
  • Secure valuables away from work zones
