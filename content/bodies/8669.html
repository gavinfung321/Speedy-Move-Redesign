Treadmill Disposal and Removal FAQ

What happens to my old treadmill after it's removed?

Once your treadmill is removed, we evaluate its condition. If it’s still functional, we prioritize donation by partnering with local charities and organizations that accept fitness equipment. This helps extend its life and provide a second-hand option for those in need. If donation isn’t possible due to its condition, we ensure that the treadmill is responsibly recycled. Treadmills contain recyclable materials like metal, plastic, and electronics, which can be recovered and reused, minimizing waste and environmental impact.

Can you remove a treadmill from any part of my home?

Yes, our team is trained to remove treadmills from any part of your home, whether it's from a basement, upstairs bedroom, or garage. We take great care when moving heavy and bulky items like treadmills, ensuring no damage is done to your walls, floors, or doorways during the process. Our experienced crew will do all the heavy lifting and maneuvering, so you don’t have to worry about a thing.

Do you recycle treadmills?

Absolutely! Recycling is a key part of our process. If your treadmill can’t be donated, we dismantle it and send the recyclable components to the appropriate facilities. Metals, plastics, and electronics are extracted and repurposed for future use, reducing the need for new materials and helping to conserve resources. We take every step to ensure the treadmill is disposed of in an environmentally responsible way.

Can you disassemble the treadmill if it doesn’t fit through the door?

Yes, our team can disassemble the treadmill if needed. Some treadmills, especially larger or older models, might not easily fit through standard doorways or staircases. In such cases, we come prepared to safely disassemble the equipment on-site. We ensure that all parts are carefully handled and that nothing is left behind after removal.

How quickly can you schedule a treadmill removal?

We strive to work around your schedule and can often arrange same-day or next-day pickup depending on availability. Simply contact us, and we’ll do our best to accommodate a time that works for you. We offer flexible scheduling to make the removal process as convenient and stress-free as possible.
