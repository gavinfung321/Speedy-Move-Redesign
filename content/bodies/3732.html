As the New Year approaches, the desire to refresh and redecorate homes often leads to the consideration of disposing of old furniture. However, rather than contributing to waste, opting for furniture recycling services can be a sustainable and responsible choice. These services not only prevent items from ending up in landfills but also contribute to the circular economy by placing recycled items for sale in the second-hand market or donating them to those in need. The Environmental Protection Department's Hong Kong Waste Reduction Website provides a valuable resource to find the right recycling service provider.

Recycling Types

Different companies specialize in various types of furniture recycling. Some may focus on office furniture, while others handle household and electrical products or even antique furniture and decorative items like mirrors. Each company may have specific requirements for items that require special handling or dismantling, such as bunk bed frames, assembled cabinets, or floor/table lamps.

Criteria

Three main criteria determine whether furniture is suitable for recycling: 1) relatively new and clean on the outside; 2) reusable; and 3) structurally safe. Companies evaluate factors like style, quantity, pick-up area, and size to decide whether to recycle items. Electrical goods should function properly for recycling. Items that are wall-mounted, custom-made, too old, damaged, or discolored are typically not accepted.

Make Arrangements Early

Some companies may take up to a week to pick up recycled items after confirmation. Consumers in a hurry to discard items should clarify arrangements. While some services are free, others may involve fees, with wait times potentially longer for free services. Clear communication about cancellations or date changes is crucial.

Cost Considerations

While some recycling services are free, larger furniture pieces may incur fees, such as $200 - $300 per piece for oversized bed frames or wardrobes. Additional charges of $50 - $100 per piece per floor may apply for staircase access. Costs can vary based on quality, quantity, pick-up area, or actual situation. Some companies may even consider paying for high-value furniture after deducting labor and other costs.

Item Verification and Potential Charges

Companies usually require consumers to provide information about furniture in advance for assessment. If the actual quality or quantity differs during pick-up, companies may charge a surcharge of $100 to $300 per piece. In some cases, companies may refuse to recycle the furniture and may charge for transportation and manpower costs.

Furniture Disposal Services

For furniture that doesn't meet recycling criteria, many companies offer furniture disposal services, but terms vary. Fees, ranging from $100 to $1,000 per piece, depend on factors like size, type, weight, pick-up area, and environment. Some companies provide disposal services only to customers who purchase their second-hand furniture.

Tips on Buying Second-hand Furniture

Apart from donating furniture, purchasing second-hand items is another sustainable option. Buyers should check if the company provides after-sales services such as maintenance and assembly. Companies may offer refunds or repairs for structural or functional issues within a specified period after the sale.

In addition to worry-free purchases, some companies provide technical guidance via telephone or instant messaging programs for assembling second-hand furniture. On-site assistance may be available for an additional fee.
