Tips: How to Rinse Cosmetic Containers

To ensure the proper recycling of cosmetic containers, use tissue paper to wipe them clean. For items like sunscreen and hand cream, avoid using water for rinsing. Instead, cut off the bottle with scissors and then use tissue paper to wipe. This precaution is especially crucial for products containing antibacterial ingredients, as water rinsing might not effectively filter out these components during wastewater treatment.

Creating a Second Life for Recyclables

Every material has its unique story and potential for a second life. The recycling process repurposes recyclables into raw materials, creating new products, or facilitating reuse. For instance:

  • Ice Packs for Food: Gel cold packs, once collected, are rinsed, sanitized, and made available for restaurants, frozen food shops, and groceries for reuse.
  • Wood Pellets: Waste wood products are recycled, and the collected wood is processed into wood pellets, contributing to sustainable energy solutions.
