Moving to a new home in Hong Kong comes with the exciting task of setting up essential utilities to ensure a comfortable living experience. To help streamline the process, here's a comprehensive guide on how to set up utilities in your new Hong Kong residence, with assistance from Speedy Move.

1. Electricity Supply:

  • Procedure: Contact the China Light and Power Company (CLP) or Hongkong Electric Company (HKE) to arrange for electricity supply.
  • Documents Needed: Proof of identity, proof of residence, and tenancy agreement.

2. Water Services:

  • Procedure: Apply for water supply services through the Water Supplies Department (WSD).
  • Documents Needed: Proof of identity, proof of residence, and tenancy agreement.

3. Gas Connection:

  • Procedure: Contact the gas company, such as Towngas, for gas connection.
  • Documents Needed: Proof of identity, proof of residence, and tenancy agreement.

4. Internet and Broadband:

  • Procedure: Choose from various internet service providers (ISPs) and schedule installation.
  • Documents Needed: Proof of identity and proof of residence.
  • Speedy Move Tip: Speedy Move can assist in coordinating internet setup for a seamless transition.

5. Landline and Mobile Phones:

  • Procedure: Select a telecom provider for landline and mobile services.
  • Documents Needed: Proof of identity and proof of residence.

6. Television Services:

  • Procedure: Choose a cable TV or satellite TV provider for television services.
  • Documents Needed: Proof of identity and proof of residence.

7. Rubbish Collection and Recycling:

  • Procedure: Familiarize yourself with the local schedule for rubbish collection.
  • Details: Hong Kong implements a Municipal Solid Waste Charging (MSW charging) system. Speedy Move can guide you through this process for efficient waste disposal.

8. Home Insurance:

  • Procedure: Explore insurance options for your new home.
  • Details: While not a traditional utility, home insurance provides essential coverage for unexpected events.

9. Change of Address Notifications:

  • Procedure: Inform relevant government departments, banks, and service providers of your change of address.
  • Documents Needed: Proof of new address and identification.

10. Emergency Services:

  • Procedure: Familiarize yourself with emergency service contacts, including the nearest hospitals and police stations.

Pro Tips:

  • Early Planning: Initiate the utility setup process well in advance of your move-in date to ensure services are ready when needed.
  • Compare Plans: Research different service providers to find the best plans that suit your needs and budget.
  • Language Support: If you're not fluent in Cantonese, inquire about English-language support when dealing with utility service providers.
