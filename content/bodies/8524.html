Can you throw a microwave in the garbage in Hong Kong?

Microwaves contain materials like metal, plastic, and electronic components, which can harm the environment if left in a landfill. Instead of throwing them away, it’s better to donate working microwaves to local charities or recycle non-functioning ones. Recycling helps keep these harmful materials out of landfills and protects Hong Kong’s environment.

Why is small appliance recycling important?

Recycling small appliances is crucial for reducing waste and environmental damage. Many contain valuable metals and materials that can be reused, minimizing the need for new production. Some appliances may even release harmful substances if left in landfills, contaminating Hong Kong’s soil and water. Recycling helps protect our local environment and ensures these resources are put to better use.

What old appliances can be recycled in Hong Kong?

Most household appliances, including air conditioners, washing machines, dishwashers, and more, can be recycled in Hong Kong. Larger appliances are often broken down for scrap metal, while smaller ones may contain recyclable materials that can be processed locally. Be sure to check local recycling guidelines to know what can go into the blue bins or be taken to recycling centers.

How should I dispose of appliances in Hong Kong?

There are several ways to dispose of old appliances in Hong Kong:
Donate: If the appliance is still functional, consider donating it to a local charity. Many organizations accept appliances and may even pick them up for you.
Sell: For items in good working order, you can sell them online through local platforms. Many buyers are willing to pick up the appliances from your home.
Recycle: Non-working appliances can be recycled at local recycling facilities. You may need to transport the item yourself, but it's worth it to reduce waste.
