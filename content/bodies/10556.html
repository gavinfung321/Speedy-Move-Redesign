Moving Process

Step 1: Assessment and Quotation

Assessment: Contact usvia phone or email to discuss your moving needs, including the size of your move, your current location, the destination, and any specific requirements you may have. Once we understand your moving requirements, our team will arrange for an assessment of your belongings and the logistics of the move.

Quotation: This assessment helps us determine the size of the moving crew needed, the type of transportation required, and any specialized equipment necessary for handling items like fragile antiques, pianos, or artwork. After the assessment, we will provide you with a detailed and transparent quotation for our moving service.

Get a Quote

Step 2: Preparation for Moving Day

Declutter: Go through your belongings and decide what you want to take with you and what you'd like to dispose of or donate. We can assist with furniture disposal if needed.

Packing: You can choose to pack your belongings yourself, or we offer professional packing services. If you prefer to pack yourself, ensure you have adequate packing supplies, such as boxes, tape, bubble wrap, and packing paper.

Step 3: Moving Day

Inventory: We will conduct an inventory of all items to be moved. Each item will be carefully labeled to ensure proper handling and placement in your new location.

Packing: If you have opted for our packing service, we'll securely pack your belongings in preparation for the move.

Disassembly: For items that require disassembly, such as furniture or large appliances, our team will carefully take them apart.

Loading: We will load your packed items onto our well-maintained and fully equipped moving vehicles, ensuring that your possessions are safe and secure during transport.

Step 4: Transportation and Unloading

Unload: Your items will be carefully unloaded from the vehicle.

Unpacking: If you've chosen our unpacking service, we'll carefully unpack and reassemble your furniture and appliances, placing them in the designated rooms.

Step 5: Post-Move Services and Sign-off

Furniture Placement: Our team will place furniture according to your specifications.

Debris Removal: We can assist with the removal of packing materials and any unwanted items.

Furniture Assembly: If furniture or appliances were disassembled for the move, we will reassemble them.

Final Inspection: Before we leave, we'll conduct a final inspection to ensure all your belongings are in the right place and in good condition. We'll have you sign off on the move to confirm your satisfaction.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Is the moving fee all-inclusive?

Our prices include basic packing, transportation, and furniture disassembly. Additional charges may apply for:

  • Staircase carrying (no elevator or over 5 flights)
  • Special items (e.g., pianos, large aquariums)
  • Remote areas (e.g., outlying islands or restricted zones)

Is there an extra charge for stairs or no elevator?

Yes, if stairs are required (no elevator), each flight costs 50–50–200 per large furniture item (depending on weight/size).

How early should I book moving services?

We recommend booking at least 7 days in advance. During peak seasons (month-end/holidays), earlier booking is advised. Express service (within 48 hours) is available for an additional fee.

Do you provide packing & furniture disassembly?

Our all-inclusive package covers:

  • Furniture & appliances: Wrapped in anti-scratch plastic film (beds, wardrobes, sofas, fridges, TVs).
  • Kitchenware: Plates/glassware packed with bubble wrap + boxes.
  • Fragile items: Extra reinforcement for mirrors/artwork.
  • Disassembly: Standard furniture (bed frames, wooden cabinets).

Do prices vary by Hong Kong district (e.g., Kowloon, New Territories)?

Base prices are citywide, but remote areas (e.g., Tung Chung, Ma Wan) incur a $200–500 transport surcharge.

How do you ensure item safety & what insurance is provided?

We use shockproof bubble wrap, anti-scratch film, and reinforced boxes, with double protection for fragile/valuable items. Full transit insurance covers up to HK$2,000 per incident.

Can you handle pianos or oversized furniture?

Yes! Our specialized piano team uses rollers and anti-shock equipment. Fees vary by type:

  • Upright piano: From $800
  • Grand piano: From $1,500

Do you provide boxes/packing materials (fees)?

Boxes are sold in sets of 10 ($200/set). We free supply bubble wrap, tape, and markers, but encourage eco-friendly reuse.

What if there’s bad weather (typhoon/rainstorm)?

No. 8 signal/Black Rain: Free rescheduling
Amber/Red Rain: Service continues (waterproof wrapping provided)

What’s your cancellation/rescheduling policy?

Cancellation: Full refund if notified 7+ days prior.
Rescheduling:1 free change (7+ days’ notice).
