Sofa Disposal and Removal FAQ

How do you donate a sofa?

Depending on where you live, different sofa donation options may be available to you. Start by researching what donation centers and charities are available in your city. If you find one you like, you can ask them about their donation process. Different charities have different requirements for donations and often require you to fill out forms, send in pictures, and wait to hear back from them.

Once you've confirmed they can take your sofa, you'll have to work out how to get it to them. If they pick it up, make sure to coordinate a day that they can come by to get it on and get a friend's help to bring your old couch outside. If not, you'll need that friend's help and a truck or large van to transport the couch to the charity.

Want to leave all that work in the hands of professionals and get rid of your old sofa today? Call Speedy Move and ask about our same-day pick up availability to get rid of your sofa fast and let experienced professionals responsibly dispose of or repurpose your old couch.

How do you take apart a sofa?

With a set of your average handyman tools and a clear area, start by removing the cushions and identifying visible and hidden screws, bolts, or fasteners. Remove those fasteners and begin disassembling the frame and arms into organized piles.
With the parts disassembled in organized piles, make sure to wrap them up in ways that set you up for success in reassembling them later. Put any small screws or washers in a plastic ziplock bag and pack away pieces of the frame in the order you disassembled them.
For all the details, check out our guide on how to take apart a couch for moving, disposal, and recycling.

Will the city pick up my old sofa?

Depending on your location, you’ll likely have one of the following options for local waste collection:
1. Schedule a pickup with your local government waste collection which will be at least 2 weeks away (in some locations, it can be as far away as 6 weeks). In most locations, you’ll also have to tell them some details about your pickup, like size and address.
2. Your local government waste collection will have designated days for large furniture and appliance pickups. These days can be as far apart as one pickup day every 3 months. You’ll have to check your local waste collection website to see how far away your next large item collection day will be.
