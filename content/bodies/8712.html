Pool Table Disposal and Removal FAQ

What types of pool tables can you remove?

We can remove all kinds of pool tables, regardless of size or weight. From small recreational tables to full-size, professional-grade models, we have the expertise and equipment to handle even the heaviest, slate-top tables. Our team is experienced in working with a wide range of pool tables, including modern and antique designs, so you can trust us to handle your table with care. No matter the complexity of the removal, we ensure a smooth process from start to finish, leaving you worry-free.

Do you handle disassembly?

Absolutely. We take care of the complete disassembly process to ensure your pool table is safely removed from your home or office. Pool tables are intricate and can be difficult to take apart due to their heavy and often delicate components like the slate, felt, and wooden frame. Our skilled team will carefully disassemble the table, ensuring that each part is handled with precision to avoid any damage to your property. We’ll not only disassemble it but also remove all pieces efficiently, saving you time and effort while ensuring a seamless removal experience.

Can the pool table be donated instead of recycled?

Yes, we prioritize finding a new home for your pool table if it’s still in good, usable condition. Many local charities, schools, or community centers welcome donations of pool tables to enhance their recreational offerings. We work closely with these organizations to facilitate the donation process whenever possible, aiming to give your table a second life. Donation helps reduce waste and allows the table to benefit others, but if donation isn’t feasible, we ensure the table is responsibly recycled.

How do you ensure eco-friendly disposal?

When donation isn’t an option, we focus on responsible recycling to minimize environmental impact. Pool tables are made of valuable materials such as wood, metal, and slate, which can all be reclaimed and repurposed. Our team works with recycling facilities to ensure these materials are separated and processed properly, keeping as much waste as possible out of landfills. By recycling pool tables, we contribute to a more sustainable process, ensuring that even old or damaged tables can be repurposed into new, useful products.

Do I need to prepare anything before the removal?

There’s very little you need to do before we arrive for your pool table removal. We’ll handle all the heavy lifting, disassembly, and transportation. However, to make the process faster and more efficient, we recommend ensuring that the path to the pool table is clear of obstacles. This allows our team to navigate through your home or office easily, reducing the risk of damage to walls or furniture. You won’t have to worry about a thing beyond this; we take care of all other aspects of the removal, from start to finish, ensuring minimal disruption to your day.
