Our Story

Our Humble Beginnings in Hong Kong

Speedy Move, a Hong Kong family business, started small with just a single truck to serve local residents and small businesses. Our commitment to providing speedy and trustworthy service soon gained recognition throughout Hong Kong, resonating with the city's tradition of hard work and diligence. With the unique blend of our family's values and the Hong Kong spirit, we began to dream bigger.

Empowering Our Family and Community

As we continued to make junk disappear for countless satisfied customers in the heart of Hong Kong, our family was about empowering others who shared our vision.

Our dream extended to creating opportunities for truck drivers and individuals who work hard for a better future. We believe in giving back to our Hong Kong community, supporting local talent and fostering growth, just as other Hong Kong family businesses have done for generations.

A Hong Kong Family Business with a Commitment to Excellence

Throughout our journey, one thing remained constant: our commitment to excellence. We uphold the traditions of Hong Kong family businesses by continually investing in our team's training and development. This ensures that every member shares our family's values of trust, reliability, and eco-friendly practices. Hard work pays off, and we've seen it firsthand, a testament to the strong work ethic deeply ingrained in Hong Kong's heritage.
