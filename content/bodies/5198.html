Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Is the moving fee all-inclusive?

Our prices include basic packing, transportation, and furniture disassembly. Additional charges may apply for:

  • Staircase carrying (no elevator or over 5 flights)
  • Special items (e.g., pianos, large aquariums)
  • Remote areas (e.g., outlying islands or restricted zones)
Is there an extra charge for stairs or no elevator?

Yes, if stairs are required (no elevator), each flight costs 50–50–200 per large furniture item (depending on weight/size).

How early should I book moving services?

We recommend booking at least 7 days in advance. During peak seasons (month-end/holidays), earlier booking is advised. Express service (within 48 hours) is available for an additional fee.

Do you provide packing & furniture disassembly?

Our all-inclusive package covers:

  • Furniture & appliances: Wrapped in anti-scratch plastic film (beds, wardrobes, sofas, fridges, TVs).
  • Kitchenware: Plates/glassware packed with bubble wrap + boxes.
  • Fragile items: Extra reinforcement for mirrors/artwork.
  • Disassembly: Standard furniture (bed frames, wooden cabinets).
Do prices vary by Hong Kong district (e.g., Kowloon, New Territories)?

Base prices are citywide, but remote areas (e.g., Tung Chung, Ma Wan) incur a $200–500 transport surcharge.

How do you ensure item safety & what insurance is provided?

We use shockproof bubble wrap, anti-scratch film, and reinforced boxes, with double protection for fragile/valuable items. Full transit insurance covers up to HK$2,000 per incident.

Can you handle pianos or oversized furniture?

Yes! Our specialized piano team uses rollers and anti-shock equipment. Fees vary by type:

  • Upright piano: From $800
  • Grand piano: From $1,500
Do you provide boxes/packing materials (fees)?

Boxes are sold in sets of 10 ($200/set). We free supply bubble wrap, tape, and markers, but encourage eco-friendly reuse.

What if there’s bad weather (typhoon/rainstorm)?

No. 8 signal/Black Rain: Free rescheduling
Amber/Red Rain: Service continues (waterproof wrapping provided)

What’s your cancellation/rescheduling policy?

Cancellation: Full refund if notified 7+ days prior.
Rescheduling:1 free change (7+ days’ notice).
