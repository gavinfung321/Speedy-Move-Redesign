Massage Chair Disposal & Removal FAQ

Do you disassemble massage chairs before removal?

Yes! Most massage chairs require partial disassembly before they can be removed. Our team brings the right tools and will handle all necessary dismantling.

Can massage chairs be donated?

If your chair is still working and in good shape, we’ll try to donate it to a local charity, elderly home, or wellness center. We aim to give items a second life whenever possible.

Are massage chairs recyclable?

Yes. Massage chairs contain recyclable materials such as metal, plastic, wires, and motors. We separate and send these to proper recycling facilities to minimize environmental impact.

Do I need to move the chair outside before pickup?

Not at all. Our full-service removal includes lifting, maneuvering, and loading the chair from inside your home. Just point, and we’ll handle the rest.

How soon can I book a pickup?

We offer flexible scheduling and can often provide same-day or next-day service depending on availability.
