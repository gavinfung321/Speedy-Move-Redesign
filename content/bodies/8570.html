Wardrobe Disposal and Removal FAQ

Do you disassemble wardrobes before removal?

Yes, we offer full disassembly services for wardrobes as part of our removal process. Our team will carefully dismantle the wardrobe before removing it from your home, ensuring no damage to your walls, floors, or other belongings.

What happens to my old wardrobe after it’s removed?

If your wardrobe is in good condition, we aim to donate it to local charities. If donation isn’t feasible, we ensure the materials, such as wood and metal, are responsibly recycled to minimize environmental impact and reduce waste.

Can you remove large wardrobes from any room in my home?

Absolutely! Whether your wardrobe is located in a bedroom, attic, or any other part of your home, our team will carefully remove it without causing damage to your property. We handle all the heavy lifting and maneuvering to make the process easy for you.

Do I need to empty my wardrobe before removal?

Yes, we ask that you empty your wardrobe of all personal items before we arrive. This makes the removal process faster and safer for our team and ensures that none of your belongings are accidentally damaged or misplaced.

Can I schedule same-day wardrobe removal?

Depending on availability, we offer same-day or next-day wardrobe removal services. Contact us to check our schedule, and we’ll do our best to arrange a convenient time for you.
