Exercise Equipment Disposal and Removal FAQ

What types of exercise equipment do you remove?

We handle the removal of various exercise equipment, including treadmills, ellipticals, stationary bikes, rowing machines, weight benches, and even entire home gym systems. Whether it's a large or small item, we're equipped to safely remove and dispose of it for you.

Do you recycle exercise equipment?

Yes, whenever possible, we prioritize recycling. Many exercise machines contain valuable materials like metal and plastic that can be reclaimed and repurposed. We ensure that the equipment is either donated to local charities or responsibly recycled to minimize environmental impact.

Do I need to disassemble the equipment before you arrive?

No, you don't need to worry about disassembly. Our professional team will handle all disassembly, removal, and transportation of your exercise equipment. We take care of everything from start to finish, so you can relax while we do the heavy lifting.

Can exercise equipment be donated?

If your equipment is in good working condition, we partner with local charities to donate it, giving it a second life in a new home. Donating is a great way to extend the life of your equipment while helping others in need.

How do you ensure no damage is done during the removal?

Our team of professionals is trained to carefully remove large, bulky items like exercise equipment without causing any damage to your home. We take precautions to protect your walls, floors, and other personal belongings while removing the equipment.
